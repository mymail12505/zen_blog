## 中文｜刹那之间，即生即空

**《仁王般若经》云：**

> 是法即生即住即灭，即有即空，  
> 刹那刹那亦如是法生法住法灭。  
> 何以故？九十刹那为一念，  
> 一念中一刹那经九百生灭，  
> 乃至色一切法亦如是。

这段经文以极其震撼的方式，揭示了**无常与空性并非抽象哲学，而是正在发生的事实**。

我们通常以为：
- 事物「存在」一段时间  
- 念头「停留」一下  
- 身体「持续」不变  

但佛法直接指出：  
**生、住、灭并非先后发生，而是同时完成。**

### 即生 · 即住 · 即灭  
### 即有 · 即空

“刹那”不是诗意的比喻，而是精确的观照单位。  
一念之中，已经历无数次生灭；  
一切色法、心法、现象，皆在崩解中显现。

所以痛苦之所以成立，  
并不是因为变化，  
而是因为我们**误认刹那为永恒**。

当你真正看见：
- 念头来时已在去  
- 身体显现时已在变  
- 世界成立时已在空  

执着自然松手，  
不是你放下，  
而是**它站不住**。

## English｜Emptiness Happening in Every Instant

**The *Prajñā of the Benevolent Kings Sutra* states:**

> All dharmas arise, abide, and cease — all at once.  
> They exist and are empty at the same time.  
> In every instant, dharmas arise, abide, and perish.  
> Ninety instants make one thought;  
> within a single instant, there are nine hundred births and cessations.  
> Thus it is with all form and all phenomena.

This passage dismantles our deepest assumption:  
that things *persist*.

What we call “existence” is not duration,  
but **continuous arising and collapsing**.

### Arising is ceasing  
### Presence is emptiness

Even a single thought contains hundreds of micro-births and deaths.  
Nothing ever stabilizes long enough to be grasped.

Suffering does not come from impermanence itself,  
but from **mistaking rapid change for solidity**.

When this is seen directly:
- Thoughts release themselves  
- Emotions lose their grip  
- The self cannot coagulate  

Not because we destroy it,  
but because **it was never fixed to begin with**.

This is not nihilism.  
This is freedom happening **now**,  
in every ungraspable instant.

---

## 结语｜Conclusion

**空性不是虚无，  
而是无法被抓住的真实。**

When you stop fighting impermanence,  
you discover that nothing was ever against you.
