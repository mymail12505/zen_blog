## 依《圆觉经》而修：四大分解，进入禅修状态

### Practicing with *The Sutra of Perfect Enlightenment*: Dissolving the Four Elements into Awareness

《圆觉经》云：**“一切众生，本来成佛；妄想执著，而不证得。”**

修行不是创造什么，而是松开误认。

> *“All sentient beings are originally Buddha; deluded thoughts and attachments prevent realization.”*

Practice is not about creating something new, but about releasing mistaken identification.

---

### 一、观身非我：四大假合

### 1. The Body Is Not-Self: The Temporary Union of the Four Elements

静坐安住，轻闭双眼，如实觉知此身。

此身由**地、水、火、风**四大暂时和合而成：

* **地大**：骨肉筋骸，终归尘土
* **水大**：血液津液，流动不住
* **火大**：体温热力，起灭无常
* **风大**：呼吸气息，来去自然

只是观照：**此身非我，乃因缘暂住。**

Sit steadily and gently close your eyes. Know the body as it is.

This body is a temporary union of **earth, water, fire, and wind**:

* **Earth**: bones and flesh, returning to dust
* **Water**: blood and fluids, constantly flowing
* **Fire**: warmth and metabolism, arising and ceasing
* **Wind**: breath, naturally coming and going

Simply observe: **the body is not “me,” but a temporary dwelling of conditions.**

---

### 二、观受是幻：感受如影

### 2. Feelings Are Illusory: Sensations as Passing Shadows

舒适与不适、轻与重，来去无常。

妄，不在感受，而在**误认感受为我**。

**感受在，觉在；感受去，觉仍在。**

Comfort and discomfort arise and pass.

Delusion is not sensation itself, but **mistaking sensation for self**.

**Sensations appear; awareness remains. Sensations fade; awareness remains.**

---

### 三、观心无住：念起念灭

### 3. Mind Abides Nowhere: Thoughts Arise and Cease

不追随，不压制，只是看。

云起云散，觉知不动。

You neither follow nor suppress—only observe.

Thoughts drift like clouds; awareness stays unmoving.

---

### 四、四大分解，觉性现前

### 4. Dissolving the Four Elements, Awareness Reveals Itself

不执身、不执受、不执念，

四大虽在，已分解于觉知。

这不是消失，而是**误认的终止**。

Releasing identification with body, feelings, and thoughts,

the four elements dissolve into awareness.

This is not disappearance, but **the end of misidentification**.

---

### 五、安住当下，不取不舍

### 5. Resting in the Present: Neither Grasping nor Rejecting

不求境界，只安住觉。

呼吸来，觉知来；呼吸去，觉知在。

Seek no special state. Rest in awareness.

Breath comes—awareness comes. Breath goes—awareness remains.

## 10 分钟引导式禅修音稿（可直接朗读）

### 10-Minute Guided Meditation Script (Read-Aloud)

**0:00–1:00｜安坐与安身**
找一个稳定坐姿，脊柱自然竖直。轻闭双眼，感受身体与坐垫的接触。

*Find a stable seat. Spine upright, eyes gently closed. Feel contact with the seat.*

**1:00–3:00｜观呼吸（风大）**
不调息，只觉知吸与呼。来，不迎；去，不送。

*Observe the breath—no control. Inhaling arrives; exhaling departs.*

**3:00–5:00｜观四大**
观骨肉为地，流动为水，温热为火，呼吸为风。它们皆在变。

*See earth, water, fire, and wind—each changing.*

**5:00–7:00｜观感受与念头**
感受起落，念头来去。你只是看见。

*Sensations and thoughts rise and fall. You simply notice.*

**7:00–9:00｜分解与回归觉知**
当不再抓取，觉知自然显现。

*Releasing grasping, awareness reveals itself.*

**9:00–10:00｜安住**
不求，不动。只是在。

*No seeking. Simply be.*
