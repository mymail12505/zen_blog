## 引言｜Introduction

**中文**  
在职场中，“权谋”常被视为生存技能，甚至是成功的必要条件；而佛教则强调不执著、不伤害、觉照当下。两者是否天然对立？还是，其实可以在更高的理解层面上并行不悖？

**English**  
In the workplace, *power and strategy* are often seen as survival tools—even prerequisites for success—while Buddhism emphasizes non-attachment, non-harm, and mindful awareness. Are these two inherently opposed, or can they coexist at a deeper level of understanding?

---

## 什么是职场中的“权谋”？｜What Is “Power and Strategy” at Work?

**中文**  
职场权谋并不只意味着阴谋或算计，它更广义地指：

> 对人性与组织结构的理解
> 对资源、信息与时机的判断
> 在复杂关系中保护自身、推进事务的能力

问题不在于 ` “有没有权谋” `，而在于**动机与方式**。

**English**  

Workplace strategy does not necessarily mean manipulation. At a broader level, it refers to:

>  [!NOTE] Understanding human behavior and organizational dynamics  
> Judging resources, information, and timing  
> Navigating complex relationships while protecting oneself and moving work forward

The real question is not *whether* power is used, but *why* and *how*.

---

## 佛教如何看待权力与手段？｜How Does Buddhism View Power and Means?

**中文**  
佛教并不否认世间运行的因果法则。佛陀本人也善于“善巧方便”（Upāya）：

- 因人说法，而非一刀切
- 顺势而行，而非逆流硬抗
- 明知世俗规则，却不被其绑架

佛教反对的不是“手段”，而是：

- 贪（为己）
- 嗔（伤人）
- 痴（无明、不觉）

**English**  
Buddhism does not deny worldly causality. The Buddha himself employed *skillful means* (Upāya):

- Teaching according to the person and situation  
- Moving with conditions rather than fighting blindly against them  
- Understanding worldly rules without being enslaved by them

What Buddhism cautions against is not *means*, but:

- Greed (self-centered gain)  
- Hatred (harmful intent)  
- Ignorance (lack of awareness)

## 在职场中如何“用而不染”？｜Using Power Without Being Tainted

**中文**  
佛教并不要求你在职场中天真或退缩，而是：

1. **看清规则**：不否认现实
2. **不主动伤人**：不以他人痛苦换取利益
3. **留有余地**：不给关系下死结
4. **随时自省**：我是在护法，还是在护我执？

**English**  
Buddhism does not ask you to be naïve at work. Instead, it encourages:

1. **Seeing the rules clearly** – without denying reality  
2. **Avoiding intentional harm** – not trading others’ suffering for gain  
3. **Leaving room** – not sealing relationships into dead ends  
4. **Continuous self-inquiry** – Am I protecting the work, or my ego?

---

## 修行不是逃离职场｜Practice Is Not Escaping Work

**中文**  
真正的修行，不在山林，而在会议室、邮件往返、权责冲突之中。

当你：

- 能觉察算计升起的那一刻
- 能选择不被情绪牵着走
- 能在复杂中保持慈悲与清醒

那一刻，职场本身就是道场。

**English**  
True practice is not found only in monasteries, but in meeting rooms, email threads, and conflicts of authority.

When you can:

- Notice the moment calculation arises  
- Choose not to be dragged by emotion  
- Remain compassionate and clear amid complexity

The workplace itself becomes a place of practice.

---

## 结语｜Conclusion

**中文**  
权谋不是修行的敌人，无明才是。  
懂世故而不世故，入红尘而不染尘，才是佛教在职场中的真正智慧。

**English**  
Power is not the enemy of practice—ignorance is.  
To understand the world without being consumed by it is the true Buddhist wisdom at work.
