# 本觉・始觉・不觉：因不觉而有轮回  
# Original Awakening, Initial Awakening, Ignorance & The Origin of Samsara

## 前言 Introduction  
禅修并不是要创造一个新的自我，也不是要追求某种神秘境界，而是回到心的真实面目。  
理解**本觉、始觉、不觉**这三个核心概念，是认识“迷”与“悟”、明白“为何轮回”的关键。

Chan meditation is not about constructing a new self or chasing mystical states.  
It is about observing the true nature of the mind.  
Understanding **Original Awakening, Initial Awakening, and Ignorance** is essential to recognizing delusion, awakening, and the origin of samsara.

---

## 一、本觉：本来具足的觉性  
## Original Awakening: The Mind’s Innate Clarity

**本觉**指心的本质：光明、清净、不生不灭，从未因迷惑而减少。  
迷只是暂时遮蔽物，如云遮日，太阳并未动摇。

**Original Awakening** refers to the inherent luminosity and purity of the mind.  
Even when buried by confusion, it never diminishes—just as clouds cannot harm the sun.

---

## 二、始觉：觉知的萌芽  
## Initial Awakening: The First Turn Toward Clarity

**始觉**是禅修中，行者开始觉察：  
- “我在生气。”  
- “这是念头，不是我。”  
- “情绪会来，会走。”  

这种对念头与情绪的辨识，就是觉醒的起点。

**Initial Awakening** arises when one starts to recognize thoughts and emotions as passing phenomena rather than the self.  
It is the moment the mind begins turning back toward clarity.

---

## 三、不觉：迷失与轮回的根源  
## Ignorance: The Root of Delusion and Samsara

**不觉**是忘失觉性，被念头、情绪、执着牵走的状态。  
人在不觉之中：  
- 把念头当实  
- 把欲望当必须  
- 把故事当自己  

**正是“不觉”构成了轮回的起点。**  
因迷而造作，因造作而受果报，因受果报而再次迷失——相续不绝，这便是轮回的循环。

**Ignorance** is the loss of awareness—being swept by thoughts, emotions, and identities.  
It is the state in which people mistake illusions for reality.  

**Ignorance is the very seed of samsara.**  
From ignorance arises action, from action arises consequence, and from consequence arises renewed delusion.  
This continuous chain forms the cycle we call *samsara*.

---

## 四、禅修的核心：从不觉、到始觉、显本觉  
## The Core of Chan Practice: From Ignorance to Awakening

禅修不是“让自己变得觉悟”，  
而是让心**从不觉回到始觉，再显本觉**：

1. **观察念头**：不压抑、不跟随  
2. **觉知当下**：看清情绪的生灭  
3. **放下执取**：不把念头当“我”  
4. **自然安住**：本觉自显，无需造作

Chan practice is not about “forcing enlightenment,”  
but allowing the mind to return from ignorance to awareness:

1. Observe thoughts without suppression  
2. Recognize emotions as transient  
3. Stop identifying with mental patterns  
4. Let awareness naturally reveal itself  

当抓取的力量松开，本觉之光自然显现。

---

## 五、结语 Conclusion  
**本觉是太阳，始觉是破云的一线光，不觉是遮蔽的云层。**  
禅修的全部道路，就是让这束光不断增长，直到遮蔽物完全散去。

**Original Awakening is the sun; Initial Awakening is the first ray breaking through; Ignorance is the cloud.**  
The entire path of Chan is the unfolding of that ray until the clouds dissolve.

May this reflection illuminate your meditation journey and deepen your understanding of mind and samsara.
