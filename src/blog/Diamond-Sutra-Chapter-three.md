引言 · Introduction

中：金刚经第三品开示菩萨修行的核心智慧——“自度为先，度人无执”。本篇浅释结合憨山大师经验，帮助读者理解佛性、无我、降伏心的实修要点。
EN: The third chapter of the Diamond Sutra reveals the core wisdom of Bodhisattva practice: "Self-liberation comes first; saving others without attachment." This annotation, drawing on Master Hanshan’s experience, helps readers understand Buddha-nature, no-self, and the essential practice of subduing the mind.

## 1. 本觉、始觉、不觉 · Original Enlightenment, Initial Enlightenment, Unawareness

金刚经原文引用：

“须菩提，凡所有相，皆是虚妄。若见诸相非相，则见如来。”

中：

本觉：众生本有佛性，本自具足觉性，但被无明蒙蔽。

始觉：修行者开始觉悟，逐渐体会佛性与空性。

不觉：尚未觉悟，被烦恼牵引，迷于五蕴与境界。

EN:

Original Enlightenment: All beings possess inherent Buddha-nature, obscured by ignorance.

Initial Enlightenment: Arises when practitioners begin to awaken, gradually realizing Buddha-nature and emptiness.

Unawareness: Those still entangled in afflictions, deluded by the five aggregates and worldly phenomena.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

观察自己心念的起伏，觉察何时心被执着蒙蔽，何时能回归本觉。

## 2 . 当一时菩萨，也不愿当一天的菩萨 · Be a Bodhisattva Without Attachment

中：
修行菩萨不为名利、时间或身份，而是真心利益众生，不计修行长短。心无挂碍，无所求，只以度己度人为本。

EN:
A Bodhisattva practices without attachment to fame, status, or duration. The heart is unbound, seeking nothing, sincerely aiming to benefit all beings.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

发心利益他人时，觉察心中是否有计较和执着，努力以无求心行菩萨道。

## 3. 五蕴身心与一切境缘不可误为实 · The Five Aggregates and Phenomena Are Not Real

中：

五蕴（色、受、想、行、识）及一切境界皆缘起，不可执为实。

四生（胎生、卵生、湿生、化生）只是众生生起的方式，本质不过是色心二法——形体与心识。

EN:

The five aggregates (form, feeling, perception, mental formations, consciousness) and all external phenomena are conditioned and impermanent, not truly real.

The four modes of birth (womb-born, egg-born, moisture-born, transformation-born) are merely ways beings arise, ultimately reducible to form and mind.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

观身心及万法如幻，练习以空观观察自己情绪和念头的起落。

## 4. 始终以色心为始，一切众生毕竟寂灭 · All Beings Ultimately Cease

中：
色心二法为万法本质，众生和诸法终究归于寂灭。生灭只是表相，心不执着生灭，即可入寂灭法门。

EN:
Form and mind constitute the essence of all phenomena. Ultimately, all beings and dharmas return to quiescence. Birth and death are appearances; by not clinging, one enters cessation.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

每日观呼吸与心念变化，体会生灭无常、心不执着。

## 5. 上求佛道，下求度众生，菩萨以自度为始 · Seek Enlightenment First, Then Save Others

中：
无自度之力，则无法度他。先自觉，再度人。自度包括修行、净心、体悟佛性；度人是利益众生，引导觉悟。

EN:
Without self-liberation, one cannot save others. Self-liberation involves practice, purification of mind, and realization of Buddha-nature. Only after self-enlightenment can one benefit others.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

反观内心，先降伏烦恼与执着，再以平等心利益众生。

## 6. 降伏其心与“实无众生得灭度者” · Subdue the Mind; Ultimately No Beings Are Saved

中：
菩萨修行以降伏心为根本。虽度无量众生，但实无众生可得灭度。若心存四相（我相、人相、众生相、寿者相），则仍有执着。真正度众生须无我相、无人相、无众生相、无寿者相，方达无余涅槃。

EN:
The essence of Bodhisattva practice is subduing the mind. Though saving countless beings, in reality, no beings are truly saved. Holding the four notions—self, person, sentient being, lifespan—maintains attachment. Only by abandoning them can one reach ultimate Nirvana and genuinely “save” beings.

禅修提示 · Practice Tip

修行度众生时，观察心中是否有“我与众生”的界限，练习无我行菩萨道。

核心总结 · Key Takeaways
中文	English
本觉为根：众生自有佛性，修行是去障显性。	Original Enlightenment as Foundation: All beings inherently possess Buddha-nature; practice removes obscurations to reveal it.
自度为先：先自觉，后度众生，度众生不执有我。	Self-Liberation Comes First: Enlighten oneself first; then help others without attachment.
无我无相：修行与度生都应“无执”，契入佛道、入无余涅槃。	No-Self, No-Form: Practice and saving others must be free of attachment, realizing Dharma and entering ultimate Nirvana.
