### 禅在未疴之前  
不积喹步，无以至千里。  
不积小流，无以成江海。  

### Before the Illness of the Mind  
Without accumulating small steps, one cannot reach a thousand miles.  
Without gathering tiny streams, one cannot form a vast sea.  

---

#### 中文  
世人多在困厄之时求悟，  
而禅，正在未疴之前。  
当一念未起，心未扰动，  
正是修行的根处。  

千里之行，从一息一念而始；  
江海之大，由涓滴不息而成。  
修行非远，  
只在当下这一念清明、这一步不怠。  

静中有悟，积中见道。  

---

#### English  
Most people seek awakening only in moments of hardship,  
yet Zen begins *before* the illness of the mind.  
When no thought arises and the heart remains undisturbed,  
there lies the root of true practice.  

A thousand miles begin with a single breath, a single step;  
the vast sea is born from countless small streams.  
Practice is not distant —  
it lives in this very clear thought, this steady step.  

In stillness there is awakening;  
in accumulation, the Way is found.
