## 中文

憨山大师云：

「若無此身，何有我執」  
「若無我執，誰為煩惱」

这两句话，如利剑直指执着的根本。

我们常以“此身”为我——  
以形体为我，以感受为我，以念头为我。  
然而细观之，这一切不过是因缘暂聚。

若无“此身”的执取，  
“我”的概念便无从建立。

进一步而言，  
若连“我执”也不复存在，  
那么所谓的烦恼，又是为谁而生？

烦恼并非外在之物，  
而是依附于“我”的认同而存在。

当“我”被看破，  
烦恼也随之失去依托。

这并非逃避世界，  
而是洞见其本质——  
无我，亦无烦恼。

在此之中，  
唯有清明觉照，如如不动。

## English

Master Hanshan said:

“If there is no body, what gives rise to attachment to self?”  
“If there is no attachment to self, for whom do afflictions arise?”

These lines cut straight to the root of attachment.

We habitually take this body to be the self—  
identifying with form, sensation, and thought.  
Yet upon close observation, all of these are merely temporary gatherings of conditions.

Without clinging to the body,  
the notion of a fixed “self” cannot stand.

Furthermore,  
if attachment to self dissolves,  
then for whom do suffering and afflictions arise?

Afflictions are not external things;  
they depend on identification with a “self.”

When the self is seen through,  
afflictions lose their ground.

This is not an escape from the world,  
but a direct insight into its nature—  
no-self, and thus no suffering.

What remains  
is clear awareness, unmoving and such as it is.
