## 中文

于中缘尘各归散灭，毕竟无有缘心可见。

这句话揭示了一种深刻的观照：一切因缘和合而生，也随因缘散灭。  
当我们执着于“有一个心”去感知、去抓取、去理解时，实际上已经落入了分别之中。

所谓“缘心”，并非真实存在的实体，而是因境而起、随境而灭的暂时现象。  
当外境消散，所谓的“心”也无从可见。

这并不是否定存在，而是指出：
存在本身即是流动、无常与空性。

在这样的体悟中，
不再执着，不再分别，
只是如实观照——来则来，去则去。

## English

Within conditions, all phenomena arise and disperse;  
ultimately, no conditioned mind can be found.

This sentence points to a profound insight:  
all things come into being through causes and conditions, and fade when those conditions dissolve.

When we cling to the idea of a “mind” that perceives, grasps, or understands,  
we have already fallen into dualistic thinking.

The so-called “conditioned mind” is not an independent entity,  
but a temporary appearance arising with circumstances and disappearing with them.

When the external conditions vanish,  
the “mind” itself cannot be found.

This is not a denial of existence,  
but a recognition that existence is inherently fluid, impermanent, and empty.

In this realization,  
there is no attachment, no distinction—  
only direct awareness: things come and go as they are.
