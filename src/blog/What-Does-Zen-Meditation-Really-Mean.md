# 禅修意味着什么？  
### What Does Zen Meditation Mean?

禅修（Zen Meditation）并不是宗教神秘活动，也不是只有和尚能做的修行。  
它是一种让心回到清醒、柔软、安宁状态的训练。

Zen meditation is not a mystical ritual nor something only monks can do.  
It is a training that helps the mind become clear, soft, and peaceful.

# 一、什么是禅修？ What Is Zen?
<a id="一什么是禅修-what-is-zen"></a>

禅修说难不难，说易不易。  
它不是要你“变成更好的人”，而是帮助你看清自己当下的状态。

Zen is neither difficult nor easy.  
It does not ask you to “become better,” but to clearly see who you are right now.

禅宗常说：  
> 心如明镜，本来清净。

但生活的压力、情绪、欲望会把“镜子”遮住。  
禅修的目的，就是让镜子再次清明。

Zen classics say:  
> The mind is like a bright mirror—pure by nature.

Life’s stress and emotions cover this mirror.  
Meditation simply cleans it.

# 二、禅修的三大核心意义 Core Meaning
<a id="二禅修的三大核心意义-core-meaning"></a>

## 1. 觉察：看见心的来去  
### Awareness: Seeing the Mind

禅修不是“赶走杂念”，而是让你看见它们。  
越想压住杂念，它越跳得厉害。

Zen is not about eliminating thoughts.  
The more you fight them, the stronger they get.

觉察，就是心的光。  
Awareness is the light of the mind.

---

## 2. 回到当下：不被拉走  
### Returning to the Present Moment

我们常常活在：  
- 过去的回忆  
- 未来的担忧  
- 自我批判  
- 与别人比较  

Most people live in:  
- memories of the past  
- worries about the future  
- self-judgment  
- constant comparison

禅修帮助我们回到真实的“当下”。  
Meditation brings us back to the real now.

---

## 3. 安定：越放越稳  
### Stability: Relax to Become Steady

越用力控制心，心越乱。  
越是放松，心越能回到清澈状态。

Trying to force the mind increases chaos.  
Relaxation brings natural stability.

---

## 小练习：数息法  
### A Simple Practice: Counting Breath

1. 吸气数 1  
2. 呼气数 2  
3. 数到 10，再从 1 开始  
4. 如果走神，轻轻回来就好  

Not to reach ten,  
but to notice when your mind has wandered.

# 三、禅修如何应用于生活？ Bringing Zen to Life
<a id="三禅修如何应用于生活-bringing-zen-to-life"></a>

禅修不是只能在寺庙或蒲团上进行。  
生活处处是禅。

Zen is not limited to temples or cushions.  
Every moment in life can be Zen.

---

## 1. 吃饭：专注味道  
### Eating: Focus on Flavors

别滑手机。  
感受香味、温度、咀嚼。

Put the phone down.  
Feel the taste, temperature, and texture.

---

## 2. 走路：觉察脚步  
### Walking: Feel Your Steps

感受脚掌着地、身体的移动。  
Feel the contact and the shifting of weight.

---

## 3. 情绪：观察不是压抑  
### Emotions: Observe, Not Suppress

愤怒、难过、焦虑来了不必推走。  
只是看见它的形状、速度、变化。

When anger or sadness arises, don’t push it away.  
Observe its shape and movement.

---

## 4. 工作：一次做一件事  
### Work: One Thing at a Time

心越专注，越轻松。  
Scattered mind = tired.  
Focused mind = ease.

---

# 四、结语 Conclusion
<a id="四结语-conclusion"></a>

禅修不是逃避，也不是追求神秘体验。  
它是一种生活方式：

**在每一刻清醒地活着。**

Zen is not escape nor mysticism.  
It is a way of living:

**Being awake in every moment.**
