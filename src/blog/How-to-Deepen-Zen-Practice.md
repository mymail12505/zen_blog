## 引言｜Introduction

**中文**  
许多人开始禅修，是为了放松、减压、改善情绪；但当修行逐渐深入，真正的问题会出现：**如何继续向内，而不是停留在表层的平静？**

禅修并不是一种技巧的累积，而是一种生命态度的转化。

**English**  
Many people begin Zen meditation for relaxation, stress relief, or emotional balance. But as practice deepens, a real question arises: **how do we move beyond surface calm and continue inward?**

Zen practice is not the accumulation of techniques, but a transformation of one’s way of living.

---

## 一、先建立稳定，而非追求体验｜Stability Before Experience

**中文**  
深入禅修的第一步，不是追求特殊境界，而是建立**稳定的练习基础**：

- 固定时间与姿势
- 不频繁更换方法
- 不以感受好坏评判修行

许多人停滞，是因为太快追逐宁静、空性或觉受，却忽略了最根本的持续性。

**English**  
The first step in deepening Zen practice is not seeking special states, but establishing **stability**:

- A regular time and posture
- Consistency in method
- Letting go of judging practice by pleasant or unpleasant experiences

Many practitioners plateau because they chase calm or insight too quickly, neglecting continuity.

---

## 二、把“觉知”带入生活｜Bring Awareness Into Daily Life

**中文**  
真正的禅修，不只发生在坐垫上。

当你开始在以下时刻保持觉知，修行才真正加深：

- 情绪升起时
- 与人冲突时
- 被赞美或批评时
- 做重复性工作的时刻

若觉知只存在于打坐中，修行就会停留在“技巧层”。

**English**  
True Zen practice does not occur only on the cushion.

Practice deepens when awareness is present during:

- Emotional reactions
- Interpersonal conflict
- Praise or criticism
- Repetitive daily tasks

If awareness is limited to sitting meditation, practice remains technical rather than transformative.

---

## 三、直面“我执”的显现｜Facing the Arising of Ego

**中文**  
随着禅修深入，“我执”不会消失，反而会变得更加明显。

你可能会发现：

- 想成为“修得好的人”
- 对进步或退步产生焦虑
- 比较自己与他人的修行

这些并不是错误，而是修行真正开始的标志。

**English**  
As Zen practice deepens, the ego does not disappear—it becomes more visible.

You may notice:

- The desire to be a “good practitioner”
- Anxiety about progress or stagnation
- Comparing your practice with others

These are not failures, but signs that practice has truly begun.

---

## 四、不逃避困顿期｜Do Not Avoid Plateaus

**中文**  
每位长期修行者，都会经历“看似没有进展”的阶段。

禅修的深化，往往发生在：

- 不再有新鲜感
- 心变得粗糙而不美好
- 只剩下枯燥与重复

若能不逃避这些阶段，反而会进入更真实、稳固的觉照。

**English**  
Every long-term practitioner encounters periods that feel stagnant.

Deepening practice often happens when:

- Novelty fades
- The mind feels dull or messy
- Practice becomes repetitive and ordinary

Remaining present during these phases allows awareness to mature.

---

## 五、从“修行”走向“如实而活”｜From Practice to Living Truthfully

**中文**  
深入禅修的标志，不是定力多强，而是：

- 是否更诚实地面对自己
- 是否更少被情绪牵引
- 是否更能承担生命的复杂

当修行不再只是“我在修”，而是**我如实地活着**，禅修便自然深化。

**English**  
The sign of deepening Zen practice is not stronger concentration, but:

- Greater honesty with oneself
- Less reactivity to emotions
- Increased capacity to hold life’s complexity

When practice shifts from “I am practicing” to **living truthfully**, Zen naturally deepens.

---

## 结语｜Conclusion

**中文**  
禅修不是为了成为某种理想状态，而是不断松开执著。

当你不再急着前进、不再评断当下，
深入，便已经发生。

**English**  
Zen practice is not about reaching an ideal state, but about continuously releasing attachment.

When there is no urgency to advance and no judgment of the present moment,  
**depth is already here.**
