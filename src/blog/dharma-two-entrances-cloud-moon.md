## I. 导论｜Introduction
中文

中国禅宗的发展以达摩祖师为宗源，而其《二入四行》提供了最早的“修行论”框架。另一方面，《大乘起信论》以“本觉、始觉、不觉”与“真如与烦恼互相熏习”的结构，奠定中国佛学理解“心性论”的核心基础。

本篇文章首次以系统方式将两者整合，展现：

禅宗修行路径（达摩）

大乘心性论结构（本觉体系）

觉悟动力学（熏习：真如/烦恼）

实修向度（止观、护念、返照）


## English

The evolution of Chinese Chan traces its roots to Bodhidharma, whose Two Entrances and Four Practices presents one of the earliest systematic frameworks for spiritual cultivation. Meanwhile, the Awakening of Faith in Mahāyāna establishes the foundational structure of original awakening (benjue), initial awakening (shijue), non-awakening (bujue), and the dynamic interplay of suchness (tathatā) and afflictions (kleśa) through mutual “perfuming” or conditioning.

This article systematically integrates these two traditions for the first time, revealing:

The Chan cultivation path (Bodhidharma)

The mind–nature framework (Awakening of Faith)

The dynamics of awakening and delusion (mutual perfuming)

Practical contemplative methods (stilling, insight, recollection)

The result is a bilingual, academically structured text suitable for courses, publications, Buddhist studies, or online media.

## II. 本觉、始觉、不觉的结构｜Structure of Original, Initial, and Non-Awakening
中文

《起信论》以三个核心概念说明“心性如何迷、如何悟”：

1. 本觉（Original Awakening）

众生本具清净自性，从未失去，只是“不相应”。本觉是“心的本底真如”。

2. 始觉（Initial Awakening）

因修行、因闻法、因回光返照而生起的“觉之初动”，是实践层面的觉悟。

3. 不觉（Non-Awakening）

无明的活动状态，是“忘失真如”的经验形式。

此三者并非分离，而是互动的“觉悟动力学”。

### English

The Awakening of Faith explains the dynamics of mind through three key concepts:

1. Original Awakening

The inherent purity of mind—never lost, but not yet realized. It is the ground of suchness.

2. Initial Awakening

The arising of insight through practice, hearing the Dharma, or turning inward. It is awakening in process.

3. Non-Awakening

The active movement of ignorance—a mode in which suchness is obscured, though never destroyed.

These three states form an interrelated dynamics of awakening.

### III. 真如与烦恼的“互相熏习”｜Mutual Perfuming: Suchness & Afflictions
中文

《起信论》的天才之处在于揭示“心的动力性”：

1. 真如内熏（Perfuming from Within）

真如不断向心识渗透，使众生具有向觉悟运动的力量。

2. 真如外熏（Perfuming from Without）

通过闻法、善友、思惟、修习，使真如的力量增强显现。

3. 烦恼熏真如（Afflictions Perfuming Suchness）

当下的妄念、执著、情绪模式，持续压覆真如，使不觉流转。

4. 真如熏烦恼（Suchness Perfuming Afflictions）

修行时，觉性之光渗入不觉，使无明逐渐松动、瓦解。

此四种熏习构成“觉与迷的双向动力结构”。

### English

The brilliance of the Awakening of Faith lies in presenting a dynamic model of mind:

1. Internal Perfuming by Suchness

Suchness continuously permeates consciousness, giving beings the potential for awakening.

2. External Perfuming by Suchness

Teachings, contemplation, and spiritual conditions amplify the influence of suchness.

3. Afflictions Perfuming Suchness

Patterns of delusion overlay and obscure the inherent clarity.

4. Suchness Perfuming Afflictions

Through practice, the light of awakening penetrates ignorance and gradually dissolves it.

These four modes form a bidirectional dynamics of awakening and delusion.

### IV. 达摩祖师的二入四行｜Bodhidharma’s Two Entrances and Four Practices
中文

达摩祖师以“理入”与“行入”开示修行全道。

一、理入（Entrance of Principle）

直观众生本具真如，从“本觉”而入，认识无我与空性。

二、行入（Entrance of Practice）

四行即修行的实践路径：

报冤行：逆境不怨，以无我观化解。

随缘行：境缘如幻，心不取著。

无所求行：离欲、离名相，安住真如。

称法行：身心随顺法性，使始觉不断增长。

### English

Bodhidharma describes the path through two entrances:

1. Entrance of Principle

Entering from the insight that all beings possess suchness — awakening starts from the ground of original purity.

2. Entrance of Practice

The four practices form the method of cultivation:

Practice of Requiting Enmity — transforming adversity through non-self.

Practice of Following Conditions — accepting phenomena without attachment.

Practice of Non-Seeking — freeing oneself from craving and conceptual identity.

Practice of Accordance with Dharma — aligning body and mind with suchness, thus strengthening initial awakening.

### V. 二入四行 × 本觉体系：整合地图｜Integrated Map
中文

将两大系统整合，可得：

本觉 = 理入的根本性

始觉 = 四行的修持过程

不觉 = 烦恼熏真如的显现

真如内外熏 = 觉悟动力

四行 = 真如熏烦恼的具体方法

形成一幅“觉悟动力 × 修行路径 × 心性结构”的完整地图。

### English

The integrated structure reveals:

Original awakening = foundation of the Entrance of Principle

Initial awakening = unfolding through the Four Practices

Non-awakening = manifestation of afflictive perfuming

Perfuming of suchness = the engine of awakening

The Four Practices = concrete methods through which suchness transforms afflictions

This becomes a complete map of mind structure × spiritual dynamics × practice path.

### VI. 结语：实践的核心｜Conclusion: The Heart of Practice
中文

悟性不是被创造的，而是被唤醒的。
迷惑不是实有的，而是习气的延续。

修行的核心——
不是“获得真如”，
而是让真如不断熏习、显现、透亮，
使始觉扩展到本觉，使不觉逐渐褪去。

### English

Awakening is not produced but uncovered.
Delusion is not substantial but habitual.

The heart of practice is not “attaining suchness,”
but allowing suchness to permeate and unfold—
where initial awakening grows into original awakening,
and non-awakening gradually dissolves.

### VII. 观察、返照与觉性的展开｜Contemplation, Reflexive Illumination, and the Unfolding of Awareness
中文

如果说“二入四行”提供修行的方法，《起信论》提供心性的结构，那么“观察与返照”便是两者之间的桥梁。

禅门之“返照”并非额外的动作，而是本觉光明自然对自身的照见。
这是：

不是“用心”

而是“心自明”

不是“努力觉知”

而是“妄念不续时，觉性自显”

返照的核心不在于“看某物”，而在于：

当心不逐境时，心的本质自显。

这正与“真如内熏”完全一致。

### English

If Bodhidharma’s Four Practices provide the method of cultivation, and the Awakening of Faith provides the structure of mind, then observation and reflexive illumination form the bridge between them.

In Chan, reflexive illumination is not an added effort; it is the natural clarity of original awakening illuminating itself. It is:

Not “using the mind,”

But “mind revealing itself.”

Not “effortful mindfulness,”

But “awareness appearing when delusion ceases to proliferate.”

The essential point is not “observing objects,” but:

When the mind does not chase conditions, its nature becomes self-evident.

This aligns directly with internal perfuming by suchness.

### VIII. 禅修的误区：用力、求境、逐念｜Common Errors in Meditation
中文

在“始觉”的阶段，修行者常落入三个误区：

1. 用力求静

以紧绷压抑妄念，只会让心更躁动。

2. 追求特境

把光相、寂静、清明当作目标，反而落入“相”。

3. 逐念以治念

试图逐念分析、判断、对治，只会延续不觉的动力。

达摩在“无所求行”中已指出：

一切境界不应求，不应著。

### English

During the stage of initial awakening, practitioners often fall into three errors:

1. Forcing the mind into stillness

Suppressing delusions leads only to more agitation.

2. Chasing meditative experiences

Taking visions or states of clarity as goals binds one to forms.

3. Fighting thoughts with thoughts

Analyzing or resisting thoughts perpetuates the cycle of non-awakening.

As Bodhidharma teaches in the Practice of Non-Seeking:

No state should be desired or grasped.

### IX. 正修与助缘：四行如何“熏习”心性｜Direct Practice and Supportive Conditions
中文

四行不仅是修行步骤，更是“真如熏烦恼”的具体方式：

四行	功能	对应熏习
报冤行	以无我化解逆境	真如熏烦恼
随缘行	心不取著境相	真如内熏显现
无所求行	断除欲望执著	减弱烦恼熏真如
称法行	行住坐卧皆随法性	真如外熏强化

当四行不断运作，始觉逐渐深化，本觉逐渐显发，不觉渐被瓦解。

### English

The Four Practices are not mere steps, but concrete modes through which suchness perfumes afflictions:

Practice	Function	Corresponding Perfuming
Requiting Enmity	Dissolving adversity through non-self	Suchness perfuming afflictions
Following Conditions	Not clinging to phenomena	Manifestation of internal perfuming
Non-Seeking	Cutting off craving and fixation	Weakening afflictive perfuming
Accordance with Dharma	Aligning daily activity with suchness	Strengthening external perfuming

Through these practices, initial awakening deepens, original awakening shines forth, and non-awakening dissipates.

### X. 本觉如何“转成”始觉？｜How Original Awakening Unfolds into Initial Awakening
中文

传统佛学认为“本觉”是已成之觉，“始觉”是未成之觉。
然而禅宗强调：

本觉不是结果，而是动力。

这意味着：

本觉是觉悟的“源头”

始觉是觉悟的“过程”

二者不是先后，而是“自性与显用”的关系

因此：

真如内熏 → 觉性自然流动

修行返照 → 始觉渐次扩大

四行实践 → 习气不断松脱

不觉减弱 → 本觉光明透显

这是一种 动态展开（dynamic actualization），而不是线性进程。

### English

Classical Buddhist texts often distinguish original awakening as “already complete” and initial awakening as “yet to be completed.” But Chan emphasizes:

Original awakening is not a result; it is the driving force.

Thus:

Original awakening = source

Initial awakening = unfolding

Their relationship is not chronological but ontological—essence and function

Therefore:

Internal perfuming → natural flow of clarity

Reflexive contemplation → expansion of initial awakening

The Four Practices → dissolution of karmic habits

Weakening of non-awakening → luminous revelation of original awakening

This is a dynamic actualization, not a linear path.

### XI. 四行与“金刚经”的空性：无我、无取、无住｜The Four Practices and the Diamond Sutra
中文

《金刚经》强调的核心是：

无我相

无人相

无众生相

无寿者相

无著、无住、无相

达摩四行正是“金刚经修行法门的生活化形式”：

金刚经核心	四行体现
无我	报冤行：无我故无冤
无住	随缘行：缘来不住
无相	无所求行：欲求即相
依法不依人	称法行：一切行归于法性

四行即《金刚经》的实践化、日常化、操作化。

### English

The Diamond Sutra teaches:

No-self

No-person

No-being

No-lifespan

No attachment, no abiding, no form

Bodhidharma’s Four Practices are the everyday operationalization of these principles:

Diamond Sutra Principle	Four-Practice Expression
Non-self	Requiting Enmity
Non-abiding	Following Conditions
Non-form	Non-Seeking
Rely on Dharma, not person	Accordance with Dharma

Thus, the Four Practices embody the Diamond Sutra in lived experience.

### 《云遮月心要经》（Pseudo-Sutra Style）

如是我闻：

心性本明，如秋月当空；  
众生迷故，随缘起云。

云来不动月，乱意不损觉。  
若见乱意非乱意，即见本心。

烦恼熏真如，则云密光隐；  
真如熏烦恼，则云薄光生。

若菩萨欲入真如，应修四行：  
逆境不起，名报怨行；  
得失不动，名随缘行；  
心无所求，名无求行；  
行合于法，名称法行。

云不起，不因风息；  
月常明，不因云现。  
知云本空，即知心明。  
明心者，见月也。

是为《云遮月心要经》。
