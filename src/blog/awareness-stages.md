# 又知又觉、不知不觉、妄知妄觉、正知正觉

在禅观修行中，众生的心识状态，大致可以归纳为四种层次：

---

## 一、不知不觉 —— 无明凡夫

凡夫众生终日随境流转，
烦恼起而不知，
妄念生而不觉。

如梦中人，
执幻为实。

此即《圆觉经》所谓：

> “一切众生，于无生中，妄见生灭。”

### 特征

- 情绪反应自动化
- 被欲望牵引
- 不觉察自己的执著
- 活在惯性之中

---

## 二、又知又觉 —— 初学观照

修行人开始学习返观自心，
妄念一起，
能够察觉。

虽然仍会起烦恼，
但已经开始“知”。

这是从无明迈向觉性的开始。

### 修行重点

- 培养正念
- 观呼吸
- 觉察情绪
- 不随妄念流转

---

## 三、妄知妄觉 —— 执著境界

修行过程中，
最危险的不是“不觉”，
而是“执觉”。

误把：

- 神秘体验
- 清净感
- 光明境界
- 空灵状态

当成开悟。

于是：

> 头上安头，
> 觉上立觉。

这正是禅宗所说：

> “知见立知，即无明本。”

### 常见误区

- 执著“我在修行”
- 执著“我已经觉悟”
- 追求特殊境界
- 用概念理解空性

---

## 四、正知正觉 —— 离幻即觉

真正的觉悟，
并非制造另一个“觉者”。

而是：

- 无住而知
- 无所得而明
- 心境一如
- 当下清净

禅宗云：

> “本来无一物，何处惹尘埃。”

此时：

觉而无觉，
知而无知。

行住坐卧，
皆是道场。

---

# 四种状态对照

| 状态 | 心识 | 特点 |
|---|---|---|
| 不知不觉 | 无明 | 随境流转 |
| 又知又觉 | 观照 | 开始觉察 |
| 妄知妄觉 | 执觉 | 执著境界 |
| 正知正觉 | 般若 | 离幻即觉 |

---

# 结语

修行不是制造一个更厉害的“我”，

而是看见：

本来没有一个固定的“我”。

当妄想止息，
圆觉自现。

# Knowing and Awakening in Zen Practice

In Zen contemplation, the states of consciousness may be understood through four progressive conditions.

---

## 1. Unknowing and Unaware

Ordinary beings drift endlessly with thoughts and emotions.

Afflictions arise,
yet they are not recognized.

Delusions appear,
yet they are not seen.

Like someone dreaming without realizing it is a dream.

### Characteristics

- Automatic emotional reactions
- Attachment to desire
- Lack of self-awareness
- Living mechanically

---

## 2. Knowing and Becoming Aware

A practitioner begins observing the mind.

When thoughts arise,
they are noticed.

Though delusions still appear,
awareness has begun.

This is the first step away from ignorance.

### Practice

- Mindfulness
- Breath awareness
- Emotional observation
- Non-attachment to thoughts

---

## 3. False Knowing and False Awakening

The greatest danger in practice
is not unconsciousness,
but attachment to spiritual experiences.

Mistaking:

- mystical visions
- peaceful states
- inner light
- emptiness experiences

for enlightenment.

Zen warns:

> “To establish knowledge upon knowledge is the root of ignorance.”

### Common Traps

- Attachment to being a practitioner
- Believing oneself enlightened
- Chasing spiritual experiences
- Conceptualizing emptiness

---

## 4. Right Knowing and Right Awakening

True awakening does not create another “awakened self.”

Rather:

- awareness without attachment
- clarity without grasping
- unity of mind and reality
- purity in the present moment

As Zen says:

> “Originally there is not a single thing.”

At this stage:

Knowing without clinging.
Awareness without self.

Walking, standing, sitting, lying down —
all become the Way.

---

# Comparison Table

| State | Condition | Characteristic |
|---|---|---|
| Unknowing & Unaware | Ignorance | Drifting with conditions |
| Knowing & Aware | Observation | Beginning awareness |
| False Knowing & False Awakening | Attachment | Clinging to states |
| Right Knowing & Right Awakening | Wisdom | Awakening beyond illusion |

---

# Conclusion

Practice is not about creating a superior self.

It is about realizing:

there was never a fixed self to begin with.

When delusion ceases,
original awareness naturally appears.
