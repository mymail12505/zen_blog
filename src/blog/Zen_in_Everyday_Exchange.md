# 龙潭崇信禅师：往来禅法与不成教禅修  
# Longtan Chongxin: Zen in Everyday Exchange



## 中文版

### 烧饼中的禅心

唐代禅师龙潭崇信，在出家之前，曾在天皇道悟禅师寺院旁卖烧饼维生。

他每天都会恭敬地供养十个烧饼给道悟禅师。  
而道悟禅师每次都会留下一个烧饼还给他，并说道：

> 「吾有余福，荫及子孙。」

崇信始终不明白：

> “烧饼本来就是我供养的，师父为何又还我一个，还说是恩惠？”

后来他终于忍不住向道悟禅师请教。

道悟禅师平静回答：

> 「汝将饼来，是汝心；  
> 我还饼去，是我心。  
> 有何不可？」

崇信闻言，当下心中震动，开始体会禅法并不离日常往来。

---

### 往来禅法：生活即修行

出家后，崇信长期侍奉道悟禅师。

但多年过去，他始终觉得：

> “师父从未真正教我禅法。”

某日，他终于向师父请问：

> “弟子来此多年，为何师父从不为我开示心要？”

道悟禅师回答：

> 「自汝到来，吾未尝不教汝。」

崇信惊讶：

> “师父何时教过我？”

道悟禅师说道：

- 你端茶来，我便接茶而饮。
- 你送饭来，我便接饭而食。
- 你合掌礼拜时，我便低头回应。

> 「何处不是教汝心法？」

崇信低头沉思。

就在这一刻，道悟禅师突然喝道：

> 「见则直下便见，拟思即差！」

崇信当下豁然大悟。

---

### “不成教”的真正意义

禅宗强调：

> 「教外别传，不立文字。」

所谓“不成教”，并不是否定经典，  
而是不执着于固定形式。

真正的禅修：

- 不在复杂理论中打转
- 不在形式里追求境界
- 不把修行变成自我执念

因为：

> 一旦落入“思量分别”，  
> 就已经离开了当下。

龙潭崇信禅师的开悟，不是在经堂里，  
而是在人与人的日常往来之中。

---

### 现代人的禅修启示

现代人最大的痛苦之一：

> 身在当下，心却不在当下。

吃饭时想着工作；  
工作时想着未来；  
休息时又想着过去。

而“往来禅法”提醒我们：

- 吃饭时，只是吃饭
- 走路时，只是走路
- 打字时，只是打字
- 呼吸时，只是呼吸

真正的禅，并不遥远。

它就在：

- 端起咖啡的一瞬间
- 回覆信息的一瞬间
- 与人微笑的一瞬间

如果能够完全觉知当下，

那么：

> 行住坐卧，皆是道场。

## English Version

### The Zen Hidden in a Rice Cake

Before becoming a monk, the Tang Dynasty Zen master Longtan Chongxin made a living selling rice cakes beside the temple of Master Tianhuang Daowu.

Every day, he respectfully offered ten cakes to the master.

Each time, Daowu would return one cake and say:

> “I leave blessings for future generations.”

Chongxin could not understand:

> “The cakes were already my offering.  
> Why return one and call it a blessing?”

Eventually, he asked the master directly.

Daowu calmly replied:

> “You bring the cakes with your mind.  
> I return one with my mind.  
> What is wrong with that?”

At that moment, Chongxin began to glimpse the essence of Zen:
the Dharma exists within ordinary human exchange.

---

### Zen in Everyday Interaction

After ordaining, Chongxin served Master Daowu for many years.

Yet he secretly felt disappointed:

> “My master has never truly taught me Zen.”

One day he finally asked:

> “Why have you never instructed me in the essential Dharma?”

Daowu answered:

> “Since the day you arrived, I have never stopped teaching you.”

Surprised, Chongxin asked:

> “When have you taught me?”

Daowu replied:

- When you bring tea, I receive and drink it.
- When you serve food, I receive and eat it.
- When you bow, I return the gesture.

> “Where is there not instruction?”

Chongxin lowered his head and began to think deeply.

Immediately Daowu shouted:

> “If you see, see directly!  
> The moment you begin thinking, you miss it!”

At that instant, Chongxin awakened.

---

### The Meaning of “No Fixed Teaching”

Zen Buddhism often says:

> “A special transmission outside scriptures,  
> not dependent on words.”

“No fixed teaching” does not reject learning or sutras.

Rather, it means:

- not clinging to rigid forms
- not chasing spiritual achievement
- not turning practice into ego

The moment the mind becomes trapped in analysis and comparison,

the living truth of the present disappears.

Longtan Chongxin awakened not through philosophy,
but through ordinary daily interaction.

---

### A Modern Practice of Zen

One of the deepest sufferings of modern life is this:

> The body is here,  
> but the mind is elsewhere.

While eating, we think about work.  
While working, we worry about the future.  
While resting, we replay the past.

The Zen of everyday exchange reminds us:

- When eating, just eat.
- When walking, just walk.
- When typing, just type.
- When breathing, just breathe.

Zen is not far away.

It exists in:

- lifting a cup of coffee
- replying to a message
- smiling at another person

When awareness fully returns to the present moment,

then:

> Every action becomes the Way.

## 延伸阅读 | Further Reflection

> 「见则直下便见，拟思即差。」  
> “Direct seeing is immediate;  
> once conceptual thinking begins, you miss it.”

龙潭崇信禅师的禅法，并不神秘。  
它只是提醒我们：

真正的清净，  
从来不在远方。

The teaching of Longtan Chongxin is not mystical.

It simply reminds us:

True clarity has never been elsewhere.
