# **Meditation Insight**  
# **等虚空界无所不遍**

## **English**

In deep meditation, the mind gradually becomes transparent, quiet, and unobstructed.  
As thoughts loosen and the sense of self softens, what remains is not an empty void,  
but a spacious clarity that holds all experiences without resistance.

This is the beginning of recognizing that consciousness is not a boundary,  
but a field — open, boundless, and ever-present.

The ancient phrase teaches:

**“As vast as space, pervading everywhere;  
The Dharma-realm is of one single suchness;  
This is the equal Dharma-body of all Buddhas.”**

*As vast as space* means the awakened mind has no edge, no center, no limitation.  
*The Dharma-realm is one suchness* reveals that all phenomena share the same fundamental nature —  
arising, changing, and dissolving within awareness.  
*The equal Dharma-body* reminds us that enlightenment is not a privilege of a few,  
but an inherent nature accessible to all beings.

Meditation, then, is not an escape from the world  
but a return to the natural clarity that was never absent.

## **中文**

在禅修的深处，心逐渐变得透明、寂静、无阻碍。  
当念头松动、自我感变轻，一切所显的不是空洞的虚无，  
而是一个宽广、明澈、包容万法的空间。

此时我们开始体会到：  
**意识并不是界限，而是一片无边无际的觉知场域。**

古语云：

**「等虚空界无所不遍，法界一相，即是一切如来平等法身。」**

“等虚空界”指觉悟之心无边无际，不被任何形相束缚；  
“法界一相”意谓一切现象皆在同一真如体性中显现；  
“平等法身”提醒我们：佛性不是特权，而是人人本具的清净本然。

因此，禅修不是逃离世界，  
而是回到从未失去的本来明净。

---

## **Closing Reflection · 余思**

To meditate is to remember who you truly are —  
not a fragment, not a self-contained identity,  
but the luminous field in which all experience arises.

禅修，就是回到真实的自己——  
不再执着为一个分离的“我”，  
而是觉察到：  
一切念起念灭，皆在本觉之中流动。
