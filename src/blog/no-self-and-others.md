<!-- ## 无自 · 自他 · 圣凡等一 · 不随文教   -->

禅者观心，见无自性。  
既无自，亦无他；  
自他不二，彼此皆空。  

圣与凡，本无差别；  
觉与迷，只是一念之转。  
当分别心息，法界圆融，  
山河草木，皆同一体。  

「不随文教」，  
非弃经论，而是不为文字所役。  
若随言语以求悟，  
终被语言所迷。  
禅之旨，在于心心相印，  
不在纸墨章句之间。  

> 🌿 无自则无他；圣凡等一，不随文教。  
> 🍃 一切众生，本来成佛。

## No Self · No Other · Oneness Beyond Teachings  

The Zen practitioner looks into the mind and sees no fixed self.  
Without self, there is no other —  
self and other are not two, both empty in nature.  

Sage and ordinary being are not apart;  
awakening and delusion differ only by one thought.  
When discrimination ceases,  
the world reveals its seamless unity —  
mountains, rivers, and all beings are one.  

“Not following written teachings”  
does not mean rejecting scriptures,  
but being free from their bondage.  
If one seeks truth through words alone,  
one is lost in words.  
Zen points directly to the heart —  
mind to mind, beyond form and language.  

> 🌿 When self is gone, there is no other;  
> 🍃 Sages and commoners are one, beyond written teachings.
