## 中文解读

佛陀告诫阿难：众生所执着的“能思能想之心”，并非真心，而是依外境而起的“虚妄心”。  
若没有六尘——色、声、香、味、触、法——此心便无从生起。  
当四大分解、外境消散，尘缘皆灭时，这个“能缘之心”也随之归于寂灭。  
由此可见，它并非常住不变之真心，而是随缘而生、随缘而灭的假相。

**禅意：**  
妄心逐境起，境灭心亦无。  
真心本不依缘，不生不灭，  
唯在万境俱息、心不随尘之处，  
方能照见其本然清净。


## English Interpretation

The Buddha said to Ānanda:  
What sentient beings cling to as the “thinking, perceiving mind” is not the true mind, but an **illusory mind** that arises only in dependence on external conditions.  
Without the six sense-objects — form, sound, scent, taste, touch, and thought — this mind cannot exist.  
When the four great elements dissolve and the sensory world disappears, both the objects and the mind that grasps them vanish together.  
Thus, this mind is not constant or real; it is a fleeting shadow born from causes and conditions.

**Zen insight:**  
The false mind rises with the world and falls when the world fades.  
The true mind depends on nothing — unborn, undying.  
Only when all appearances rest and the mind no longer follows dust,  
can one glimpse its pure, original nature.
