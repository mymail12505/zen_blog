## 恰恰用心时 · 恰恰无心用  

禅者言「无心」，非无觉、无想，  
而是心无挂碍，不被用心之念所缚。  

恰恰用心时，恰恰无心用；  
当我们刻意去“修”、去“悟”、去“做”，  
心即分成能与所，境便不圆。  

然若放下用心之意，  
行中自然有觉，动中本来无碍。  
无心恰恰用，常用恰恰无。  
动静一如，随缘任运，  
此即无功之功，无修之修。  

> 🌿 真修无修，真用无用。  
> 🍃 心若无心，法法圆成。

## When You Try to Use the Mind, It Is No-Mind That Works  

In Zen, *no-mind* does not mean blankness or absence,  
but a state free from grasping, free from effort.  

When you *try* to use the mind,  
you have already divided subject and object —  
you stand apart from what is.  

Yet when you let go of trying,  
awareness flows naturally through every act.  
No-mind acts precisely, without hesitation.  
Action and stillness are not two;  
the Way moves by itself.  

> 🌿 True practice is no practice.  
> 🍃 When the mind is no-mind, all things are complete.
