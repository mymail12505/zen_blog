# 🪷 禅修三昧

禅修的目的，并非仅仅追求身心的放松与片刻的宁静。  
真正的禅修，是以入道为始，以求道为路，以悟道为归。  

当人了知一切皆幻，便能离幻；离幻之时，即是觉悟之时。  
此乃禅修三昧之要义。  

---

# 🪶 Zen Samadhi

The purpose of Zen meditation is not merely to seek relaxation or tranquility.  
True meditation begins with entering the Path, continues through seeking the Way, and culminates in realization.  

When one perceives illusion as illusion, one is already free from it;  
and when illusion is left behind, awakening naturally arises.  
This is the essence of Zen Samadhi.
